About MyFarmSight

MyFarmSight is the all-in-one poultry management platform designed around the real challenges layer farmers face every day. Track your flock, connect with a local veterinarian in seconds, and has a built-in staff training that fosters continuous learning and skill enhancement. Everything your farm needs lives in one powerful platform. We are a complete ecosystem.

Key features

2.1 Flock management system

  • Farmer or farmer-owner and farm staff interface
  • Create an account and onboard seamlessly
  • Add a batch and assign staff
  • Track daily activities such as:
    • Egg production recording and analysis
    • Feed and water consumption monitoring
    • Mortality records
    • Farm income and expense recording
    • Sick birds isolation record
    • Medication usage record
    • Crack egg record
    • Environmental management e.g. temperature record
    • Farm performance reports and analytics
    • Staff and farm-management functions
    • Notification alert
    • Analytics such as laying cycle overview, tracking and monitoring feed usage, and financial analysis

2.2 Instant connection with a local veterinarian

  • Create an account as a veterinarian
  • Verify your account using your veterinarian council license identification number (e.g. Veterinary Council of Nigeria “VCN”)
  • Update your services for visibility by farmers
  • Chat with farmers and provide services
  • Earn while you provide services

2.3 A learning platform that prioritises the human capital development of farm staff

  • It is designed to prioritise the development of farm staff.
  • It comes with simple, well-organised video learning modules that equip staff with the knowledge and skills needed for efficient poultry farm operation.
  • It accommodates individual differences, making it relevant, relatable and accessible to staff across educational levels.

Features may be introduced, modified, suspended or discontinued as the Service develops. Also, use of some of these features depends on the farmer-owner's subscription package.
