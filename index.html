About Us

MyFarmSight is a complete ecosystem for poultry farmers.

MyFarmSight is a product developed by SIAGROPAT LIMITED. A company focused on revolutionising agricultural practice through a simple, innovative farming approach.

Built for Everyday Challenges

MyFarmSight brings everyday poultry operations into one place. Track flock activity, follow farm performance, and connect with qualified veterinarians when expert guidance is needed.

Accessible Professional Care

Farmers can find and consult veterinarians about flock health, disease management, and productivity. Vets can offer services directly through the platform, helping farmers get professional guidance without unnecessary delays.

Everything your farm needs resides in one powerful platform, “MyFarmSight”

Our platform creates a more efficient, informed, and responsive poultry production system, reducing delays in care while improving both poultry health and operational results.

Why MyFarmSight?

Every Farmer who keeps proper records has a holistic view of their farm and earns more

Data doesn't lie. Farms using digital management tools see dramatic improvements in profitability, efficiency, and staff accountability.

Platform Features

Everything You Need to Run a Profitable Farm

From daily operations to professional networking, MyFarmSight covers every inch of your farm management needs.

Real-Time Dashboard

Monitor daily operations live, including livestock counts, mortality, production, and income, all visualized in intuitive infographics updated in real time.

💰

Financial Management

Track stocking costs, feed expenses, sales revenue, and weekly/monthly summaries. Know exactly how profitable every batch is.

Feed & Water Monitoring

Log daily feed consumption, water quality checks, and stock levels. Get automated reminders when feed is running low or water tanks need cleaning.

Health & Medication Records

Maintain a complete sick bay log, medication administration, isolation records, and vet consultations. Never miss a treatment cycle again.

Staff Management & Verification

Collect staff bio-data, guarantor info, and next-of-kin digitally. Identity verification helps ensure every team member is accountable, verified, and trusted.

Analytics & KPI Dashboards

Weekly and monthly performance summaries. Compare batch-to-batch results, identify trends, and make informed decisions backed by your own data.

🔔

Smart Alerts & Reminders

Automated alerts for feed reorders, tank cleaning and litter pushing so nothing slips through the cracks.

How It Works

A Clear Path for Every Professional

Whether you manage a farm or provide veterinary care, MyFarmSight gives you the tools to get started and grow.

For Farmers

Organize your farm and make informed decisions.

1

Create your account

Sign up as a farmer or agricultural professional. Verify your identity and set up your farm profile in minutes.

2

Create your Animal House

Create and organize animal houses for easy tracking and management.

3

Add Your Livestock

Record the poultry batches and add your staff.

4

Track Daily activities

Log feed, water, production, health, and farm finances.

5

Grow with Data

Use analytics, access to veterinarian to continuously gain insights to improve and scale your farm.

For Veterinarians

Reach farmers and deliver professional care.

1

Set up your account

Create your veterinarian account on MyFarmSight.

2

Register your business information

Add your business details to your profile.

3

Describe your expertise

Give a brief description of your expertise so farmers can learn more about you.

4

Verify your VCN number

Verify your Veterinary Council of Nigeria (VCN) number.

5

Offer your services

Offer your services to farmers and start earning.
