About Us

MyFarmSight is a complete ecosystem for poultry farmers.

MyFarmSight is a product developed by SIAGROPAT LIMITED. A company focused on revolutionising agricultural practice through a simple, innovative farming approach.

Built for Everyday Challenges

MyFarmSight is an all-in-one poultry management platform designed around solutions to the real challenges poultry farmers face every day. Our platform tracks your flock's daily activities and connects poultry farmers with qualified veterinarians, making expert help easy to access whenever it’s needed.

Continuous Staff Development

In addition, the platform includes a built-in staff training model that supports continuous learning and skill development. The quality of work delivered by farm staff is directly proportional to their understanding of the job, so ongoing training helps improve performance, reduce errors, and strengthen overall farm outcomes.

Accessible Professional Care

Veterinarians can join the platform and offer their services, while farmers can quickly find, consult, and get guidance on flock health, disease management, and productivity. This means faster responses to health issues, better decision-making, and improved outcomes for poultry operations, without the usual delays or barriers to accessing professional care.

Together, this creates a more efficient, informed, and responsive poultry production system, reducing delays in care while improving both poultry health and operational results.

Everything your farm needs lives in one powerful platform, “MyFarmSight”.

Platform Features

Everything You Need to Run a Profitable Farm

From daily operations to professional networking — MyFarmSight covers every inch of your farm management needs.

Real-Time Dashboard

Monitor daily operations live — livestock counts, mortality, production, and income — all visualized in intuitive infographics updated in real time.

💰

Financial Management

Track stocking costs, feed expenses, sales revenue, and weekly/monthly summaries. Know exactly how profitable every batch is.

Feed & Water Monitoring

Log daily feed consumption, water quality checks, and stock levels. Get automated reminders when feed is running low or water tanks need cleaning.

Health & Medication Records

Maintain a complete sick bay log — medication administration, isolation records, and vet consultations. Never miss a treatment cycle again.

Staff Management & NIN Verify

Collect staff bio-data, guarantor info, and next-of-kin digitally. For Nigerian users, NIN verification ensures every team member is accountable and trusted.

📍

Location-Based Pro Network

Discover verified veterinarians near you. Chat in real time, request consultations, and leave reviews — all within the app.

Analytics & KPI Dashboards

Weekly and monthly performance summaries. Compare batch-to-batch results, identify trends, and make informed decisions backed by your own data.

⭐

Reviews & Rating System

Build trust across the ecosystem. Rate veterinarians after service. The platform rewards integrity and quality service.

🔔

Smart Alerts & Reminders

Automated alerts for feed reorders, tank cleaning, medication schedules, staff birthdays, and follow-up visits — nothing slips through the cracks.
