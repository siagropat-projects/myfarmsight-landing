Platform Features

Everything You Need to Run a Profitable Farm

From daily operations to professional networking — MyFarmSight covers every inch of your farm management needs.

📊

Real-Time Dashboard

Monitor daily operations live — livestock counts, mortality, production, and income — all visualized in intuitive infographics updated in real time.

💰

Financial Management

Track stocking costs, feed expenses, sales revenue, and weekly/monthly summaries. Know exactly how profitable every batch is.

🌾

Feed & Water Monitoring

Log daily feed consumption, water quality checks, and stock levels. Get automated reminders when feed is running low or water tanks need cleaning.

🏥

Health & Medication Records

Maintain a complete sick bay log — medication administration, isolation records, and vet consultations. Never miss a treatment cycle again.

👥

Staff Management & NIN Verify

Collect staff bio-data, guarantor info, and next-of-kin digitally. NIN verification ensures every team member is accountable and trusted.

📍

Location-Based Pro Network

Discover verified veterinarians, labs, and agri-professionals near you. Chat in real time, request consultations, and leave reviews — all within the app.

📈

Analytics & KPI Dashboards

Weekly and monthly performance summaries. Compare batch-to-batch results, identify trends, and make informed decisions backed by your own data.

⭐

Reviews & Rating System

Build trust across the ecosystem. Rate veterinarians, labs, and other farmers. The platform rewards integrity and quality service.

🔔

Smart Alerts & Reminders

Automated alerts for feed reorders, tank cleaning, medication schedules, staff birthdays, and follow-up visits — nothing slips through the cracks.

How It Works

Up and Running in Minutes

MyFarmSight is designed to be simple enough for any farmer, powerful enough for enterprise operations.

1

Create Your Account

Sign up as a farmer or agricultural professional. Verify your identity and set up your farm profile in minutes.

2

Add Your Livestock

Record your animals, batches, and farm details. Add your staff with digital bio-data and NIN verification.

3

Track Daily Activities

Log feed, water, production, health events, and financials each day. The dashboard updates automatically.

4

Grow with Data

Use analytics, professional connections, and AI-powered insights to continuously improve and scale your farm.
